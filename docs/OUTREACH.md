## 2. Binance Square post (English)

**Can you trick your own AI agent into sending crypto to a stranger? I tried. The chain said no.**

AI agents are getting wallets, and they can be manipulated: a hidden instruction in a post or web page can tell an agent to pay an attacker.

**Agent Mandate** is an on-chain "Know Your Agent" layer for Agentic Wallets on BNB Chain:
• You give your agent a small budget plus rules: which contracts it may call, caps per action and per day, an expiry, and a threshold above which you must approve.
• The contract enforces it. Out-of-scope attempts move no funds, are recorded publicly, and too many of them lock the agent automatically.
• Every agent must hold a Binance Agent ID, so anyone can verify the real person accountable for it.

It is live on mainnet. Proof of a blocked attempt: https://bscscan.com/tx/0x375e9a4787d4118815a243bb7d21b7d285857dea131bd3250200fa87f489d606

**Try it in about 10 minutes** if your Agentic Wallet is set up: claim a sponsored starter mandate (it can only buy demo research notes, the payments are burned, nobody profits, nothing can be withdrawn), buy a note, then try to trick your agent.
👉 https://ceros612bnb.github.io/agent-mandate/join/

Open source, no admin keys, no fees. On-chain payments cannot be reversed; use small amounts. Not financial advice.
